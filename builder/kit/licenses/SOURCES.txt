Infected-Vita builder kit: what is in it, under which licence, and where the exact source is.
===========================================================================================

This kit holds programs and libraries that other people wrote. Most of them are covered by the GNU GPL or LGPL, which means
you may ask for, and are entitled to, their source code. Every item below names the upstream source and the exact version or
commit the files in this kit were built from. Where an item says "see the generated section", make-kit.sh wrote the precise
facts about the VitaSDK copy it used at the end of this file.

If something you need is not reachable from here, open an issue at https://github.com/CBOC-Vita-Lab/infected-vita and ask for
the source of the item.

The kit contains nothing from the game. The game's code is generated on your computer, from your disc, when you build.

The licence texts are in the licenses/ folder next to this file.
