# Variants for vita/tools/ab_session.sh: one line each. The pairs are run A, B, A, B, ... (the round count is --rounds) so that the
# run-to-run noise of about 1 fps lands on both sides alike.
#
#   base: <config.txt lines every run gets, separated by ;>
#   pair: <name> | A: <lines> | B: <lines>          (A is the baseline; the lines are config.txt lines separated by ;)
#
# Every switch of the November round is here once, off against on (B is the new behaviour, as shipped by default). Switches that do not
# exist in the build under test are harmless: config.txt keys nobody reads are ignored. Add your own lines freely.
