Infected for PS Vita - Version 1.1
==================================

Thanks for trying the native Vita port of Infected! It runs the game
directly on the Vita (no emulator) at 30 frames a second, and the
CBOC Vita Installer puts it on your Vita from your own disc in about
10 minutes.


What you need
-------------
- A Vita or PS TV with HENkaku or Enso, and VitaShell
- The CBOC Vita Installer (version 2.0 or newer), for Windows 64-bit,
  macOS (10.13+ on Intel, 11+ on Apple silicon) or Linux 64-bit:
  https://github.com/CBOC-Vita-Lab/vita-installer/releases/latest
- Your own Infected disc image: US version, ULUS-10054, 1.02 (.iso)
- About 1 GB of free disk space on the computer while it builds

There is no ready-made game to download: the package contains code
translated from your disc, so the installer builds it on your own
computer. Nothing is uploaded.


Install in five steps
---------------------
1. Dump your UMD. A Vita has no UMD drive, so use a PSP 1000, 2000 or
   3000 with custom firmware such as ARK: put the UMD in, press SELECT
   on the home screen for the VSH menu, set USB Device to "UMD Disc",
   open Settings > USB Connection, and copy the .iso to your computer.
   (CSO and PBP files do not work.)
2. Open the installer on your computer and VitaShell on your Vita
   (press SELECT in VitaShell so the installer can find it).
3. Pick Infected and choose your .iso. The installer checks it at once.
4. Press Install, then Allow. It downloads the builder (40-50 MB, once),
   builds the game in about 10 minutes and sends it to your Vita.
5. Open the Infected bubble on the Vita's home screen.

If the installer offers "Prepare your Vita", that installs ShaRKBR33D
(it needs Wi-Fi on the Vita) for the optional shader compiler.


Another way
-----------
The release also has the same builder for each computer, infected-build.
On Windows drag your .iso onto it; elsewhere run: infected-build Infected.iso
It writes infected.vpk. Copy that to the Vita and install it in VitaShell,
or choose it in the installer. Then copy your ISO into ux0:data/infected/
(the installer does this for you). Details: INSTALL.md


Updating
--------
Install the new version the same way, or install the new VPK over the
old one. Your saves and settings stay.


While you play
--------------
- Settings menu: hold SELECT + START, or tap the top-right corner
- Leave the game: hold START + SELECT + L + R for a second
- Fire with R, lock on with L, map on the right stick (Modern layout)
