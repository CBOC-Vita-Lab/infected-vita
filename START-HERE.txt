Infected for PS Vita - Version 1.0
==================================

Thanks for trying the native Vita port of Infected! It runs the game
directly on the Vita (no emulator) at 30 frames a second.


What you need
-------------
- A Vita or PS TV with HENkaku or Enso, and VitaShell
- libshacccg.suprx in ur0:data/ (the shader compiler vitaGL games use)
- Your own Infected disc image: US version, ULUS-10054, 1.02 (.iso)
- A PC to build the game package once (about 20 minutes)

There is no ready-made VPK to download: the package contains code
translated from your disc, so you build it yourself.


Install in four steps
---------------------
1. Build infected.vpk from your disc: see docs/BUILDING.md
2. Copy infected.vpk to the Vita, open it in VitaShell, press Cross
3. Copy your ISO into ux0:data/infected/
4. Open the Infected bubble


Updating
--------
Install the new VPK over the old one. Your saves and settings stay.


While you play
--------------
- Settings menu: hold SELECT + START, or tap the top-right corner
- Leave the game: hold START + SELECT + L + R for a second
- Fire with R, lock on with L, map on the right stick (Modern layout)
